Frequently asked questions
Everything you need to know about CampaignDesk

How is my campaign's data kept private?

Each campaign has completely isolated data. Your member lists, meeting notes, and strategy documents are never shared with other campaigns — even on the same platform. We use database-level separation (campaign_id) and enforce strict access controls. No campaign can see another's data. Period.

Who owns my campaign data?

You do. 100%. You can export all your member data, meeting records, and activity logs at any time. If you cancel your subscription, you get a full export before we delete anything. We don't sell or share your data with anyone — including political parties or third parties.

Where is my data hosted?

All data is hosted in Kenya on local servers. This means faster access for your team, and full compliance with Kenyan data protection laws. No international data transfers unless you explicitly request a backup export.

Can my supporters see internal campaign strategy?

No. The public noticeboard only shows what you choose to publish — updates, event announcements, and pledges. Your internal team dashboard, member roster, meeting minutes, and strategy notes are completely private and require team login credentials.

What happens if I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Changes take effect immediately. Upgrade and you get access to new features right away. Downgrade — you keep access to your data but lose higher-tier features. We'll never delete your data without giving you 30 days notice and an export option.

How do team members log in?

Each team member gets their own phone number and access code. You can add or remove team members from your dashboard anytime. Secretaries have full access, agents can manage members, coordinators can view schedules — all role-based.

Is there a free plan?

We don't offer a free plan because campaigns need reliable, supported software. We do offer a 14-day free trial on all plans so you can test everything before committing. No credit card required to start the trial.

🔒 Our data privacy commitment

We treat campaign data with the same care we'd want for our own. Each campaign's data is fully isolated, encrypted at rest, and accessible only to authorized team members. We never share data between campaigns, with political parties, or with third parties. Your strategy, your member lists, your meetings — they stay yours. Period.
